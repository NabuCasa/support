  position: 40
  labels: green, troubleshooting

- No. Home Assistant Green does not have any onboard radios.
- For [Zigbee](https://www.home-assistant.io/connectivity/zigbee/) or [Thread](https://www.home-assistant.io/connectivity/thread/), you can use a [Home Assistant Connect ZBT-1](https://www.home-assistant.io/connectzbt1).
- For Bluetooth, you can use [an ESPHome as a Bluetooth proxy](https://esphome.io/projects/?type=bluetooth) or add a USB dongle and use [local Bluetooth support](https://www.home-assistant.io/connectivity/bluetooth/#installing-a-usb-bluetooth-adapter).
