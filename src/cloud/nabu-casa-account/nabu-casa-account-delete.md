If you want to delete your Nabu Casa account, you need to cancel your subscription first.

## To cancel the subscription and delete the account

1. Cancelling your Home Assistant Link subscription.
   - If you are currently subscribed to Home Assistant Link, you need to cancel your subscription before deleting your account.
   - Log in to your [Nabu Casa account](https://account.nabucasa.com/), and under **Plan management**, select **Cancel plan**.
   - **Result**: the page shows the date when your Home Assistant Link subscription will end.
     - The end date depends on your subscription type (monthly or yearly).
     - After this period, you will lose access to the following functionality:
       - Secure remote access to your Home Assistant
       - Easy integration with popular voice assistants
       - Off-site storage of your latest backup
2. If you have connected Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant through Home Assistant Link, disconnect them.
3. To delete your Nabu Casa account, select **Account settings**.
   - In the **Danger** section, select **Delete your account**.
   - **Result**: If you deleted your account while there was still time left on your subscription, your account is not deleted immediately. Instead, it will be scheduled for deletion once your subscription expires.
