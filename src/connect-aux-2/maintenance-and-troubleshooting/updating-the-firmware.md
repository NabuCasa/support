Home Assistant Connect AUX-2 updates over the air through ESPHome. When a new version is available, Home Assistant shows an update notification. Select **Update** to apply it.

To run your own firmware, see [Custom firmware and restoring factory firmware](/hc/en-us/articles/40528961462941).
